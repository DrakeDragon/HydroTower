# Hydro Tower

*Let the water flow. Raise your tower. Turn every drop into fortune.*

## About the Game

**Hydro Tower** is a relaxing incremental idle game that combines animated water physics, hydroelectric machinery, resource management, collectible water sources, and long-term tower progression. Starting with a single stage, players earn coins as water flows through machinery, reinvest their earnings into upgrades, and gradually construct a towering cascade of 30 individually named stages.

Water tumbles down angled chutes, spins paddle wheels and turbines, passes through timed sluice gates, swirls through whirlpools, and splashes into a reservoir below. Players can actively accelerate their earnings by tapping or simply watch their growing tower operate automatically.

## Core Gameplay

- Earn coins continuously from every unlocked tower stage.
- Tap the playfield to create bursts of water droplets and collect immediate bonus income.
- Purchase new stages to extend the tower upward and increase production.
- Improve machinery and water intake through seven upgrade paths.
- Discover collectible Springs that permanently increase income and change the water's appearance.
- Evaporate the tower to earn Clouds, then rebuild with permanent bonuses.
- Return after time away to collect offline earnings.

There are no combat encounters, lives, or time-limited levels. Progress is driven by investment decisions, increasingly valuable machinery, and the satisfaction of watching the tower grow.

## Eight Water and Machinery Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Chutes | Alternating sloped channels guide water through zigzagging paths. |
| Wheelhouse | Funnels direct water into rotating paddle wheels, sometimes with a second wheel. |
| Turbine Basin | Narrow basin outlets channel water toward turbines. |
| Cascade | Successive stone ledges create stepped waterfalls. |
| Jet Flume | Directional jets propel water along a flume. |
| Whirlpool | Swirling currents redirect droplets through a funnel. |
| Sluice Gate | Timed gates periodically interrupt and release water flow. |
| Splash Garden | Rows of pegs scatter droplets into changing paths. |

The visible water uses simulated droplets affected by gravity, obstacles, currents, and machinery. Wheels respond to actual droplet impacts and display animated rotation and glows. Some stages allow water to pool before draining. Coin production follows the game's economic progression rather than measuring actual energy from each droplet.

The five production upgrades multiply together, while Rain Tap rewards active play and Reservoir supports longer idle sessions. Six capped upgrade paths provide 125 purchasable ranks in total, alongside the uncapped Rain Tap path.

A quick-purchase panel recommends a production upgrade or tower expansion based on affordability and estimated return. It shows the cost, expected income increase, and savings progress.

## Evaporate — Permanent Progression

Evaporation is Hydro Tower's prestige system. Once the earnings threshold is reached, players can exchange their current tower progress for Clouds. Each Cloud adds 12% to all income permanently, with the Clouds combining into an additive bonus multiplier.

Evaporating returns the player to Stage 1 with no coins or regular upgrades, while preserving collected Springs, selected water color, Clouds, and Spring purchase history. New Cloud rewards depend on earnings and existing Clouds; the first earnings threshold is two million coins.

The system encourages repeated rebuilding and increasingly powerful production runs, even after completing the full 30-stage tower.

## Idle Earnings and Saving

The tower generates income automatically during play. When returning after more than one minute away, players receive offline income at 50% of their saved production rate, subject to an offline duration limit. The initial limit is two hours and can be extended to 24 hours through Reservoir upgrades.

Progress saves automatically in the browser, including coins, tower stages, upgrades, collected Springs, and Clouds. No account is required.

## Visual Experience and Controls

Hydro Tower presents a full-screen vertical playfield with dark jewel-toned backgrounds, metallic machinery, colorful water droplets, glowing wheel hubs, animated currents, and floating coin rewards. Each stage has a distinct color palette, while collectible Springs allow players to personalize the water itself.

The interface keeps the current coin balance, income per second, and visible stage name at the top. Four bottom menu tabs provide access to Upgrades, Tower, Springs, and Evaporate without permanently covering the machinery.

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Collect bonus coins and splash water | Tap or click the playfield |
| Explore the tower | Drag vertically |
| Purchase improvements | Upgrades or quick-purchase panel |
| Unlock or revisit stages | Tower menu |
| Discover or select water colors | Springs menu |
| Restart with permanent bonuses | Evaporate menu |
